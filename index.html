What We Offer

Comprehensive Transport Solutions for Modern Families

From GPS-powered live tracking to verified drivers and flexible plans — everything your family needs, built into one platform.

Live GPS Tracking

Watch your child's bus move in real-time on an interactive map. Accurate to within 10 meters, updated every 5 seconds.

Verified Drivers

Every driver undergoes criminal background checks, medical fitness tests, CDL verification, and annual retraining programs.

Smart Notifications

Receive push alerts when your child boards, when the bus is 5 minutes away, and when they safely arrive at school.

Optimized Routes

AI-powered route planning minimizes travel time, reduces fuel consumption, and ensures the safest path for every student.

360° CCTV Monitoring

HD cameras cover every angle inside and outside the bus. Footage is securely stored and accessible to authorized parents.

Flexible Subscription Plans

Monthly, termly, and annual plans tailored for single students, siblings, and schools. Online payment with downloadable receipts.
