Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026 · Effective: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

SafeRide Schools, Inc. ("SafeRide," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families who use our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, and school transport services (collectively, the "Services").

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and all applicable federal and state privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create a parent or guardian account, we collect: your name, email address, phone number, home address, payment information (processed securely by Stripe), and school/district information.

2.2 Student Information

To provide safe transport services, we collect limited student data including: first and last name, school grade level, assigned bus stop, Smart ID card number, and boarding/alighting timestamps. We do not collect any unnecessary personal information from students.

2.3 Location Data

Our GPS tracking system collects real-time location data from buses in our fleet. This data is used exclusively to provide live tracking to parents and is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

2.4 CCTV Footage

Bus-mounted cameras record footage for safety purposes. Footage is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored for 30 days, and accessible only to authorized parents (for their child's area of the bus), school administrators, and law enforcement upon legal request.

2.5 Usage Data

We automatically collect technical information including: browser type, IP address, pages visited, session duration, and app usage patterns. This is used solely to improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the SafeRide transport platform
  • To send boarding confirmations, arrival notifications, and emergency alerts
  • To process subscription payments and issue receipts
  • To respond to customer support inquiries
  • To conduct safety investigations when incidents are reported
  • To comply with legal obligations and law enforcement requests
  • To send optional newsletters and product updates (with consent)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors who process payments (Stripe), send notifications (Firebase), and host our infrastructure (AWS)
  • Schools & Districts: We share transport logs with enrolled schools to verify student attendance
  • Law Enforcement: When legally required by court order or legal process
  • Emergency Responders: When a child's safety is at immediate risk
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with user notification

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SafeRide takes the privacy of children extremely seriously. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. All student information is provided by parents or guardians during enrollment. Parents have the right to review their child's data, request corrections, and request deletion at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@saferideschools.com or call +1 (800) SAFERIDE.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your data:

  • 256-bit AES encryption for all data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 for all data in transit
  • SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure (AWS)
  • PCI DSS Level 1 compliance for payment processing
  • Multi-factor authentication for all admin access
  • Annual third-party penetration testing

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw marketing consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@saferideschools.com with your request.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login sessions and core functionality
  • Performance Cookies: Anonymous analytics to improve the platform (Google Analytics)
  • Preference Cookies: Remembering your theme and language preferences

You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary: account data for the duration of your subscription plus 12 months; CCTV footage for 30 days; booking records for 7 years (for tax compliance); and anonymized analytics data indefinitely.
