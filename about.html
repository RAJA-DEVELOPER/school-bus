Our Journey

From One Bus to a City-Wide Smart Transport Network

What started as a single bus route in 2009 has grown into one of the region's most trusted, technology-powered school transport platforms.

View Our Services Contact Team

2009 — The First Route

Founded by educators turned entrepreneurs, SafeRide launched its first route with 3 buses and 47 students in Springfield, IL.

2013 — GPS Integration

We became the first school bus operator in the state to install real-time GPS tracking and launch a parent notification app.

2017 — CCTV & Smart ID

360° camera systems and student ID boarding confirmation launched across our entire fleet, setting new safety benchmarks.

2021 — Electric Fleet Pilot

Launched 12 electric buses in partnership with school districts, reducing emissions by 40% on pilot routes.

2024 — 12,000+ Students

Now serving 47 routes, 12,000+ daily students, and 18 school districts across 3 counties with a 98.7% safety record.
