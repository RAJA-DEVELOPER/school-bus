Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about enrollment, drivers, and safety.

Visit our Subscription Plans page, choose your plan, and complete the online enrollment form. Enrollment takes under 5 minutes. Your child will receive their Smart ID card within 3 business days.
We respond to all inquiries within 4 business hours (Mon–Fri 6AM–8PM CST). For urgent matters, please call our main line or use the emergency hotline for active transport issues.
Safety concerns are treated with highest priority. You can report via the Parent Dashboard (Report Issue button), call our main line, or email safety@saferideschools.com. All reports are reviewed within 2 hours and escalated to our Safety Director immediately.
Premium and Family plan subscribers can submit route change requests through the Parent Dashboard. Our team reviews and responds within 2 business days. Stop changes depend on available route coverage in your area.
