What We Offer

Six Core Services That Set Us Apart

Every service is designed around one priority — your child's safety, comfort, and on-time arrival.

Live GPS Tracking

Military-grade GPS updates every 5 seconds. See your child's bus on an interactive map, track speed, route progress, and ETA to your stop in real-time.

Verified Drivers

Every driver passes a 7-stage verification: background check, CDL validation, medical fitness, safety training, drug screening, road test, and annual renewal.

Smart Parent Alerts

Push notifications when the bus is 5 min away, when your child boards (with ID scan confirmation), and when they safely arrive at school.

360° CCTV System

HD cameras cover every interior and exterior angle. Footage is securely encrypted, timestamped, and accessible for 30 days by authorized parents and staff.

AI Route Optimization

Our AI analyzes 47 routes daily, adjusting for traffic, weather, and school schedules to minimize travel time and maximize safety on every run.

Emergency Support

Dedicated 24/7 emergency hotline staffed by trained coordinators. One-tap SOS from the parent app connects directly to dispatch and local emergency services.

The Process

How SafeRide Works — From Enrollment to Every Journey

A seamless four-step flow built to guarantee total security and parent convenience.

1

Enroll Online

Choose your plan, enter your child's details, and select your nearest bus stop in under 5 minutes.

2

Get Assigned

We assign your child to the optimal route and bus, match them with a verified driver, and issue their smart ID card.

3

Track Live

Access the parent dashboard to watch the bus approach your stop, receive arrival alerts, and confirm boarding.

4

Arrive Safely

Receive school-arrival confirmation, check the daily log, and access CCTV footage if ever needed. Complete peace of mind.
